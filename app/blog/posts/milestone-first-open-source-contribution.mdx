On January 16th, 2025, my first open source contribution was merged and deployed to [nextstrain.org](https://nextstrain.org), an award-winning online platform to visualize the spread of pathogens.

## The Problem

As described in the original [issue](https://github.com/nextstrain/nextstrain.org/issues/962), the navigation bar was rendering correctly on browsers but not mobile devices. 

## The Solution

Instead of making the navigation bar collapsible, the project team advised hiding the Nextstrain title in order to free up enough space for the Login button to be visible. Since the team is in the middle of a [code migration](https://github.com/nextstrain/nextstrain.org/issues/1052), I updated both the [legacy component](https://github.com/nextstrain/nextstrain.org/blob/40be39bae236785e5fd477c4877ed1090c53da88/static-site/src/components/nav-bar/index.tsx) and the migrated [component](https://github.com/nextstrain/nextstrain.org/blob/40be39bae236785e5fd477c4877ed1090c53da88/static-site/components/nav/index.tsx) with the @media query fix.
